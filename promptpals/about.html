About PromptPals

PromptPals is a free set of small tools that turn a few simple answers into a clear, well-structured prompt for AI chat apps like ChatGPT and Claude.

How it works

Pick a tool, fill in the fields, and press Generate. You get a ready-to-use prompt that you can copy, edit, save, or open directly in an AI app. The prompts are built in your browser, so nothing you type is sent to our servers.

Credits

Every visitor gets 500 free credits each month, plus 50 back for each new day they visit. Bigger tools cost more credits than small ones. Credits are stored in your own browser and reset on the 1st of each month.

Why we built it

Good prompts get better answers, but writing them from scratch is slow. We wanted simple tools anyone can use for everyday tasks like emails, studying, planning meals, and budgeting.
